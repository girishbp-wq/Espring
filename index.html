Technology

How eSpring works

eSpring combines three powerful technologies in one compact system: a compressed carbon block filter, UV light purification, and an intelligent monitoring system.

Carbon block filtration

The carbon block removes particles as small as 0.2 microns, significantly reducing chlorine, particulates, pesticides, industrial pollutants and more — while retaining beneficial minerals.

UV light purification

A UV lamp inside the unit inactivates 99.99% of waterborne bacteria and viruses, without adding any chemicals or affecting taste.

Smart monitoring

An electronic chip tracks water usage and time, ensuring the system maintains performance and alerts you when it’s time to replace the cartridge.
